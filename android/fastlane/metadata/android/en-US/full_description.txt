Gorget connects your phone to a private network that you run yourself. Nothing ever leaves your own server: there is no account with us, no analytics, no crash reporting and no external fonts or libraries phoning home.

<b>What you get</b>
* Direct, encrypted connections between your devices (WireGuard), with automatic NAT traversal and a relay fallback that works on networks that block UDP
* Optional protection against future quantum computers between your Gorget devices
* Exit nodes: send all internet traffic through a device you choose, with a kill switch
* Per-app routing: choose which apps use the VPN
* Device names that work everywhere (laptop.gorget.internal), shared subnets and split DNS
* Always-on VPN, quick settings tile, home-screen widget, Android TV support

<b>You need</b> a Gorget server (open source, AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/anand34577/gorget

The app is signed with the project's own key; checksums and signatures are published with every release.
